22Why did the students plan to use a cowboy film excerpt in their display?

to provide a historical context for jeans
to show how the design of jeans was adapted
to illustrate why jeans originally became popular

答案
A

解析
录音中解释用电影片段是为了让观众感受当时美国穿牛仔裤的那种背景环境，也就是提供相关的历史背景。

干扰
你可能会因为听到后面说“大家都喜欢牛仔电影”而过度推断，实际上提到电影就是为了展示当时的穿着环境。

句子
Then you're going to show how jeans became increasingly popular in the eighteen-forties, and you're going to use a short clip from a cowboy movie for that. So what made you decide to do that? Well, the movie's very recent, but in fact, the costume designs ==**are accurate for the period,**== and it'll give our audience a feel for the sort of setting where jeans were worn at that time in America. And anyway, nearly everyone enjoys cowboy movies.

24What item from 1873 did the students find in a museum?

an original pair of jeans
an early design for a pair of jeans
an old photograph of someone wearing jeans

答案
A

解析
录音中提到他们在博物馆非常幸运地找到了一“条”刚好是1873年那个年份的真实牛仔裤实物。

干扰
录音说博物馆只允许他们拍照片带走，你可能会把这个“自己拍的照片”误听成了选项里的“老照片”。

句子
And we went to the museum because we wanted to see if they had something from eighteen seventy-three, because that was the year when Levi Strauss took out a patent on his design for jeans. And we were really lucky because they had a pair from that exact same year. They couldn't let us take them away, of course, but they did let us take a photo of them.
