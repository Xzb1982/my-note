12Recent American studies found that confidence will

sometimes reduce people's chances of success.
be increased by reading self-help books.
ensure good results on business courses.

答案
A

解析
录音提到一个人可能会“自信过头（too much confidence）”且单靠自信不足以保证成功，这就说明自信有时反而会降低人们成功的机会。

干扰
你如果听到“买书（buying...books）”和“保证成功（guarantee success）”就去选B或C，说明你漏听了前面的否定词“isn't enough”和后面的负面评价“wasting their time（浪费时间）”。

句子
But according to recent American business school studies, believing in yourself isn't enough by itself to guarantee success. Confidence can be a good thing, but a person can have too much confidence. And people who try to learn to be confident by buying those how to succeed books are just wasting their time.


16What does Arthur say about taking risks?

He advises being cautious.
He favours a long-term view.
He believes it is financially advantageous.

答案
A

解析
Arthur个人认为在面对短期风险时，最好还是“小心行事（tread carefully）”，这就表明他在建议大家保持谨慎。

干扰
如果你只抓听到了“short-term”就反向推测选B（长期目光），或者听到“increase profits”就选C，说明你没有听到他说被利润冲昏头脑容易“失去的比得到的还多（losing more than you gain）”这一核心警告。

句子
Then there's the question of taking risks, even calculated ones. I personally feel it's actually better to tread carefully when it comes to taking short-term risks. You need a degree of vision, certainly, but it's easy to get carried away by your desire to increase profits. And if things go wrong, you can end up losing more than you gain.

21What part of Kathy’s dissertation has the tutor just read?

her results section
her introductory chapter
her review of the literature

答案
B

解析
导师明确表示他刚看完女生论文的“第一章（first chapter）”，这直接对应了选项B的“引言章节（introductory chapter）”。

干扰
你可能会听到最开始女生提到的“literature review”或者导师建议下个月完成的“results section”就急着选，但这俩都不是导师“刚刚看完（has just read）”的内容。

Remember, you should aim to have your results section completed by the end of next month. So there’s plenty of time to finalize the whole thing by the summer deadline. Anyway, looks like you’re on track. I’ve been through your first chapter now and I was quite impressed.

23Kathy and the tutor agree that she needs to

do some more library research.
record more data.
narrow down her topic.

答案
B

解析
导师建议女生采取一些“额外的测量（additional measurements）”，女生也表示同意，这对应了选项B的“记录更多数据（record more data）”。

干扰
女生自己随后提到了需要做更多图书馆研究（library work），你极有可能会想选A，但导师紧接着就否定了这个想法（don't think that's essential），做题一定要听完双方最终达成共识的部分。

句子
However, I think you’d get more convincing results if you took some additional measurements. I’ve noted exactly what I mean on your work. Yes, I thought that might be necessary and I know I need to get down to some more library work too. Well, I don’t think that’s essential at the moment.

24Why does the tutor give an example from his own experience?

to show how successful he has become
to illustrate how times have changed
to encourage Kathy to do something similar

答案
C

解析
导师讲完自己年轻时主动请教教授的经历后，明确表示他“强烈建议所有学生抓住此类机会（urge all my students to take any such opportunity）”，也就是鼓励女生去效仿，对应选项C。

干扰
听完这个故事后，女生抱怨了一句“时代变了（times have changed）”，你很容易被带偏去选B，但这只是女生的吐槽，并不是导师讲故事的初衷。

句子
And you never know how successful a chat with him might turn out to be. I got my first research post when I was a final year student and asked a visiting professor to explain what he meant in some article he’d written. So I urge all my students to take any such opportunity that arises. But times have changed. People like that are too busy to talk to mere students these days.
