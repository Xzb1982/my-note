4Female giraffes tend to live longer than males.

TRUE
FALSE
NOT GIVEN

答案
C

解析
文章只说雌性长颈鹿能活20年甚至更久，但根本没有把它们的寿命和雄性进行比较。

干扰
你可能看到提到了具体的年龄“20年”，但这不代表有对比，没有提到雄性能活多久就果断选NOT GIVEN。

句子
Female giraffes can live 20 years or more, and it makes sense they might rely on each other for clues to the best feeding grounds, help with taking care of their young, or the reduction of stress, by staying in groups.


6Younger bulls hold their necks higher ==**in the presence of more powerful males.**==

TRUE
FALSE
NOT GIVEN

解析
原文说年轻公鹿遇到更强大的老鹿时，会立刻试着让自己显得矮小无辜，而不是把脖子抬得更高。

干扰
你可能只看到了14句的“把头抬高”，但那是它们单独待着的时候，15句有个“But”转折，说明遇到强大的公鹿它们就认怂了。

句子
Recent observations show young bulls, when left on their own, mimicking their elders: head held high and neck puffed out. But should a dominant older bull come into view, the younger males instantly try to make themselves look small and innocent.

birth

答案
birth

解析
原文说如果小鹿脖子太长，长颈鹿妈妈就很难分娩，所以脖子在出生后才长长，为的是让妈妈更容易生产（birth）。

干扰
题目里的“easier”对应原文的避免“difficult time giving birth”，所以提炼出分娩这个动作的名词“birth”即可。

句子
The growth of the neck largely takes place during early childhood, as giraffe mothers would have a difficult time giving birth to young with longer necks.
