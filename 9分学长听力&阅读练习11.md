25  26Which TWO books do Jen and Rob decide to get from the library?

A. Voice Identification
B. Modern Forensic Techniques
C. Language and Crime
D. Forensics Today
E. Research Methods in Forensic Linguistics

答案
B

解析
女生在排除完其他选项后，明确说“我们应该借出《Modern Forensic Techniques》”，对应了从图书馆借阅的动作。

干扰
你可能会因为听到《Voice Identification》（选项A）也很有用就想选，但女生紧接着明确表示这本书是向同学George借，而不是从图书馆借。

句子
"Research Methods in Forensic Linguistics, I guess we ought to borrow that, and Voice Identification will be a useful one. I'll borrow that from our classmate, George. He won't need it for his project. But we should get out Modern Forensic Techniques. We need all the help we can get [laughs] on that subject."



29-30Which TWO sections of the project will Rob write alone?

A. Methodology
B. Literature review
C. Introduction
D. Analysis
E. Discussion and conclusions


答案
C

解析
女生问男生是否擅长写“引言（introductions）”，因为自己总是写不好，男生一口答应说“交给我吧（leave that to me）”。

干扰
你可能听到了“文献综述（Literature review，选项B）”，但紧接着女生就建议两人把这部分“平分（split that part）”来写，而不是交给男生单独写。

句子

"Now, Rob, let's decide how we're going to split the writing between us. Are you any good at introductions? They always seem to let me down. Okay, leave that to me. Are you okay to do the literature review then? Maybe we should split that part."

The Vikings - 答题回顾

答案

poems

写笔记

解析

他们的历史和故事通过**诗歌（poems）**被保存和传承下来。

干扰

"Passed down through..." 对应原文的 "transmitted through..."。干扰项 "oral traditions" 是两个词，且出现在前一句。

句子

Speaker: These narratives were preserved and transmitted through poems, which kept their cultural heritage alive for generations.
