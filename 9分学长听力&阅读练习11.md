month

写笔记

解析

录音直接指出每个月（every month对应each month）学生们都必须要进行考试（do tests）。

干扰

你可能会因为听到句末的year而填错，但year是指记录表现的一整年周期，测试的频率其实是每个月。

句子

And every month, rather than the teacher giving the children a report, as I know some schools do, they have to do tests, and we record their performance over the year.
