The History of the Pencil V2 - 答题回顾

5Pencil production in the Borrowdale area only lasted until the 1860s.

TRUE
FALSE
NOT GIVEN

答案
B

解析
原文说1860年代后只是修改了设计，并且紧接着说当地至今仍在制造铅笔（still manufactures pencils today），所以“只持续到1860年代”是错的，选 FALSE。

干扰
你可能会被1860s这个时间点骗到，但紧接着下一句就推翻了停产的说法，做判断题一定要看完整段语义。

句子
English pencils continued to be made with sticks cut from pure Borrowdale graphite into the 1860s, after which the design was modified. The town of Keswick, close to the Cumberland graphite find, still manufactures pencils today, the factory also being the location of the Cumberland pencil museum

The origin of language - 答题回顾

![[Pasted image 20260919124231.png]]
G和E选反了
答案
E


解析
Derek Bickerton 认为语言的前身是由缺乏语法的“词（words）”串在一起组成的，这对应选项E“早期语言由基本词汇组成，使用方式不系统（无语法）” 。

干扰
虽然他在讲早期语言，但他认为这种语言依然是由一个个“词（words）”构成的，千万不要和后来 Alison Wray 认为“没有单个词（no individual words）”的观点搞混淆了 。

句子
But Bickerton's idea that the precursor of modern language consisted of words strung together with limited, if any, grammar is, I believe, fundamentally mistaken. (第7段)

答案
G

解析
Alison Wray 认为早期语言是由“信息（messages）”构成的整体，而“不是由更小的意义单位（即词汇）组合而成的”，这对应选项G“早期语言没有单个词汇” 。

干扰
这里的“not composed out of smaller units of meaning (i.e. words)”是解题的绝对核心，明确否定了单个词汇的存在 。

句子
By this she means that the precursor to language was a communication system composed of 'messages' rather than words... But in Wray's proto-language, they were not composed out of smaller units of meaning (i.e. words) which could be combined together using a rule system (i.e. grammar)... (第8段)
