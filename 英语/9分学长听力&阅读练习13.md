8The Bom Jesus was owned by a group of private Portuguese traders.

TRUE
FALSE
NOT GIVEN

答案
B

解析
原文明确表示这艘船的主人是国王若昂三世（King João III），而不是私人商人。

干扰
你可能看到第一句的trading ships就误以为是私人商人的船，但做题必须定位到针对Bom Jesus号具体所有者的描述。

句子
Among the vessels was the Bom Jesus, or "Good Jesus", a recently built ship ==**owned by King João III**==. It was commanded by Dom Francisco de Noronha and carried around 300 sailors, soldiers, merchants, priests, nobles and enslaved people.

10A surviving report written by the fleet commander provides a detailed account of how the ship sank.

TRUE
FALSE
NOT GIVEN

答案
B

解析
原文一开头就明确说明“没有关于这场灾难的完整记录留存下来”，直接推翻了题目中“有详细记录”的说法。

干扰
你可能会因为后面详细列出了沉船的过程而认为是舰队指挥官写的，但那其实是研究人员推测重建（reconstructed）出来的。

句子
Although no complete account of the disaster survives, researchers have reconstructed a likely sequence of events. Approximately four months after leaving Portugal, the fleet encountered a violent storm near the Cape of Good Hope, and its ships became separated.
