As our legal system is constantly improving, there is a voice which suggest that it is necessary to send lawbreakers into jail.On the opposite, other people recommend that lawbreakers who  have better talents, ought to assign some works to them. 

I mostly agree with the first idea.On one hand,  Restricting illegal behaviours can punish the evils and raise the kindnesses.It is necessary to restrict these behaviours in order to creating a promising social atmosphere.Similarily , sending the outlaws into the prison help children to form a proper moral judgement.On the another hand, by legally restricting the outlaws in prison , we can defend our legal system efficiency .

I disagree with the second one , because limiting the lawbreakers personal freedom can prevent them from continously threatening  the victims and others.The outlaws may commit crime intentionally ,so restricting them by sending into jail can help the criminals review their detrimental behaviours  . In addition, it can protect the legal rights of the victims .(人身自由 personal freedom)

Furthermore, I deem that if the lawbreakers who have talents , can work under some regulations and contribute their values to the society.This measurement may reduce  governence cost and give the criminals another opportunities to turn over a new leaf. In conclusion,although we can let the criminals to contribute their values for practial purpose, lawbreakers should face the deserved punishments in order to warning others and upholding the justice.
