Pacific Tapa Cloth - 答题回顾

24Large pieces of tapa are made from smaller pieces which are

stuck together
woven together
sewn together

答案
A

解析
录音中提到大块的布是用一种糊状黏合剂把不同的小块“粘（glued）”在一起的，完美替换了选项A的“stuck together”。

干扰
录音里确实抛出了“编织（woven）”和“缝合（stitching）”这两个干扰词，但紧接着就否定了它们，说树皮太细不能编织，缝合也不够结实，千万别听到选项原词就急着选。

句子
So with the production process itself, first the inner bark is beaten with wooden hammers to soften the fibers. Then the different pieces are glued together using an adhesive paste made from the arrowroot tuber. This is the only way to fabricate large pieces of cloth because bark strands are too fine to be woven together, and stitching isn't strong enough.

adhersive adj.可粘贴的，粘性的
