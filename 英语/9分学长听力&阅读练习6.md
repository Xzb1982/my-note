Elephants And Farms - 答题回顾

25What is result of the experiment of hanging a container of bees by the farms?

the trees with bees were trampled down by elephants
the elephants started to look for the trees with bees
it can be used for protecting farmer's crops

答案
C

解析
实验发现，大象会远远地躲开那些挂了蜜蜂容器的树，说明这个方法能有效防住大象，从而“被用来保护农民的庄稼”，对应选项C。

干扰
如果只捕捉到了“踩踏（trampled down）”这个词，你极容易误选A，但仔细听就会发现大象踩踏的恰恰是“没有（didn't have）”挂蜜蜂的树。

句子
They hung containers full of bees in trees near the farms. And they listened in trees without containers? And they found the elephants trampled down the trees that ==**didn't**== have any containers. And stayed well away from the ones with the bees in.

26How did the elephants respond to the audio recording of bees?

they were not aware of the danger
they ran away quickly and kept looking back
they stopped and looked for the source of the sound

答案
B

解析
录音生动描述了大象听到声音后，朝着反方向“狂奔（dashed off）”并且“不停回头看（kept turning their heads around）”，完全契合选项B。

干扰
听到“停下（stopped）”时你可能会着急选C，但大象停下并非为了“寻找声音来源”，它们随后的动作是迅速逃跑，很容易因为抓错动作细节而掉进陷阱。

句子
Well, they've got a recording of a swarm of angry bees, and they played it to herds of elephants out in the wild, and they all stopped what they were doing immediately and stood erect with their heads up and their ears extended. They must have been really aware there was danger somewhere. But not the sort of danger that they can face up to and charge. So they dashed off in the opposite direction. They kept turning their heads around.
