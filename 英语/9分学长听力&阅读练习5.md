Early Approaches to Organisational Design - 答题回顾

14 15   According to the writer, which TWO of the following are characteristics of the classical approach to organisational design?

==**A. a marked ranking order for employees**==
B. giving importance to everyone’s work
~~C. the advancement of older workers~~
==**D. a neutral working environment**==
E. increased benefits for workers

答案
D

解析
B段提到韦伯（Weber）认为这种组织的特征之一是“impersonal climate（非个人化的氛围）”，即不掺杂个人情感的中立环境。

干扰
==**“Impersonal”在这里不是“冷漠无人性”的贬义，而是指“对事不对人/客观中立”，对应选项D的“neutral”。**==

句子
(Paragraph B) "According to Weber, such organisations were characterised by... an impersonal climate."

According to Weber, such organisations were characterised by the specialisation of tasks, appointment by merit, and an impersonal climate.
