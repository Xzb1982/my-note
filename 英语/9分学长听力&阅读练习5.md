9Venus flytraps are better adapted to the soil of swamps and bogs than other carnivorous plants.

TRUE
FALSE
NOT GIVEN

答案

C

写笔记

解析

题目比较捕蝇草和“其他食肉植物”谁更适应沼泽土壤。原文F段只说了食肉植物整体比“传统植物（conventional plants）”有优势，==**没有在食肉植物内部进行比较**==，选 NOT GIVEN。

干扰

文中确实提到了适应沼泽，但比较对象是“普通植物”，而非“其他食肉植物”。

句子

(Paragraph F) “...so carnivorous plants enjoy an advantage there over ‘conventional’ plants.”

10Carnivorous plants frequently find it difficult to photosynthesise in bogs due to a lack of sunlight.

TRUE
FALSE
NOT GIVEN

答案

B

写笔记

解析

题目说食肉植物在沼泽中因“缺乏阳光（lack of sunlight）”难以进行光合作用。原文F段说沼泽通常“flooded with sunshine（阳光充沛）”，选 FALSE。

干扰

“Flooded with sunshine” 直接反驳了 “lack of sunlight”。

句子

(Paragraph F) “Also, bogs are often flooded with sunshine, so even an inefficient carnivorous plant can carry out enough photosynthesis to survive.”
