Eyesaver Charity - 答题回顾

15What is the main purpose of this program?

to show people their donation can make a difference
to show people how easily eye problems can be cured
to educate people about common eye diseases

答案
A
解析
该计划的目标是让人们意识到，即使是最小的捐赠也能改变一个人的生活（change a person’s life），即造成影响/差别。

干扰
C 选项（教育人们常见眼病）是**下一个项目（next programme）** 的目标；B 选项（展示眼病容易治愈）是人们在街上感到惊讶的事，并非该项目的宗旨。

句子
Speaker: Our goal is to make people aware that even the smallest of donations can help change a person’s life.
